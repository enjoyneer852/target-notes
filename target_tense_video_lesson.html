Target Plus P.6-T1-L9 Grammar Summary

1. Simple Present Tense 簡單現在式 習慣 / 事實

用法：永恆情況/事實、習慣、喜好、狀況

動詞：原本動詞 (⚠️ He/She/It 動詞加 +s/es/ies)

Keywords: always, usually, sometimes, often, seldom, once a week, twice a month, every day, on Mondays...

例句： Biana goes shopping every Sunday.
2. Simple Past Tense 過去式 過去已完成

用法：過去了，現在已改變

動詞：轉過去式 (went, played, saw)

Keywords: yesterday, ago, last week/month, this morning, today (需細心判斷是否已過去)

例句： Biana went shopping yesterday.
3. Simple Future Tense 將來式 將有可能發生

用法：將有可能發生的動作或計劃

動詞：will + 原本動詞 (will go)

Keywords: coming Sunday, soon, later, tomorrow, next year/week...

例句： Kelvin will go to Japan next week.
4. Present Perfect Tense 現在完成式 過去帶到現在

用法：由過去帶到現在，過去動作影響現在行動

動詞：has / have + p.p. (Past Participle)

Keywords: already, just, never, ever, yet, before, since + 起點, for + 時期

例句： I have moved to Japan since 2010.
