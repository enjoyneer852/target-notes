Target English - P.6-T1-L9 Grammar Note

時態總整理 (Simple Present / Past / Future / Present Perfect)

1. Simple Present Tense (簡單現在式)
  • 用法：永恆狀況/事實、習慣、喜好、狀況
  • 動詞：原本動詞 (原本不變)
    ⚠️ 留意: He/She/It 後動詞要加 +s / +es / +ies
  • Keywords: always, usually, sometimes, often, seldom, once a week, twice a month, every day/month, on Mondays...
  • 例句：Biana goes shopping every Sunday.
2. Simple Past Tense (過去式)
  • 用法：過去動作、現在已改變
  • 動詞：轉過去式 (went, played, saw...)
  • Keywords: yesterday, ago, last (Monday/week), this (morning/afternoon - 需判斷是否已過), today
  • 例句：Biana went shopping yesterday.
3. Simple Future Tense (將來式)
  • 用法：將有可能發生
  • 動詞：will + 原本動詞
  • Keywords: coming (Sunday/week/weekend), soon, later, tomorrow, next (year/week/month)...
  • 例句：Kelvin will go to Japan next week.
4. Present Perfect Tense (現在完成式)
  • 用法：由過去帶到現在，或過去動作影響現在
  • 動詞：has / have + p.p. (Past Participle)
  • Keywords: already, just, never, ever, yet, before, since + 起點 (since 2009), for + 時期 (for 2 years)
  • 例句：I have moved to Japan since 2010.
Target Plus Sir/Miss 提提你： 考試做卷時，一定要先尋找句中的 Keyword (提示字)！找不到提示字時，要細心判斷說話時動作是「習慣」、「過去」定係「由過去維持到現在」！
